This Privacy Policy describes how Virtual Roll Call collects, uses, and handles your information when you use the Virtual Roll Call mobile application and visit any of our hosted website pages.

Virtual Roll Call is a private, non-public-facing enterprise utility designed exclusively for authorized government agencies, public sector clients, and their designated personnel.

1. Information Collected and How Virtual Roll Call Uses It

Virtual Roll Call only collects information that is necessary to provide, secure, and maintain our application services.

Profile Information

  • Data Collected: Your name and email address.
  • Purpose: This information is used to uniquely identify you as a user, personalize your experience, and allow you to manage your account.

Device Information and Session Data

  • Data Collected: Your device's IP address.
  • Purpose: Virtual Roll Call uses your IP address to generate a unique session token. This token keeps you securely logged into the app, maintains your active session, and protects your account from unauthorized access.

Media and User-Generated Content (UGC)

  • Data Accessed: On-demand access to your device’s native Camera and Photo Library.
  • Purpose: Virtual Roll Call only accesses these features when you explicitly initiate an action (such as tapping the media attachment buttons) to upload imagery to a post or comment. Images are transmitted to Virtual Roll Call's secure servers solely to display your posts and comments to your authorized department network. Virtual Roll Call does not scan, harvest, or utilize your photos for any external purpose.

Biometric Data (Face ID / Touch ID)

  • Data Accessed: iOS Biometric Authentication framework
  • Purpose: If enabled by you, Virtual Roll Call utilizes the native iOS Keychain service to securely store your login credentials locally within your device's hardware secure enclave for fast auto-population. Virtual Roll Call never accesses, collects, logs, or transmits your biometric data.

2. Website Hosting and Technical Data

Virtual Roll Call's Privacy Policy and Support Contact pages are hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit these pages, GitHub may automatically collect and store technical information in server log files for security and operation purposes.

  • Information Collected: This information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, internet service provider (ISP) data, and timestamps.
  • Purpose of Collection: GitHub processes this data strictly to maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and ensure website stability.
  • No Marketing Tracking: GitHub does not use this data for marketing or behavioral tracking.
  • Data Privacy: For more details, please review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Government Utility, Record Retention, and Account Deletion

Because Virtual Roll Call operates as an official communications tool for public sector and government entities, data lifecycles are governed by enterprise rules and statutory mandates.

  • Account Lifecycle & Management: User profiles, department groups, and organizational assignments are centrally provisioned, maintained, and deleted exclusively by the Virtual Roll Call organization and your agency's designated system administrator. In compliance with enterprise B2B provisioning models, there is no public self-service registration or direct account deletion mechanism inside the application interface.
  • User-Initiated Content Deletion & Regulatory Auditing: Users possess the operational ability to delete posts and comments they have personally authored from the active live application feed. However, because this application functions as an official tool subject to public records laws, statutory record-retention policies, and internal affairs protocols, all communication data—including deleted posts or comments—remains securely logged within a read-only administrative archive. This data is accessible solely to authorized agency administrators for official compliance tracking and audit trails, and is never exposed to third parties.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing Disclosure

Virtual Roll Call values your privacy and maintain strict control over your personal and technical data.

  • No Data Brokering: Virtual Roll Call does not sell, rent, trade, or share user profiles, device information, uploaded media, or session tokens with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: Virtual Roll Call does not participate in cross-app tracking. Virtual Roll Call does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Internal Infrastructure Only: Your data is processed exclusively within Virtual Roll Call's secure enterprise infrastructure to provide and maintain the application's core communication functions. Your device data is only transmitted to our trusted hosting provider (GitHub Pages) when you view this document or Support Contact pages, as described in Section 2.

5. User-Generated Content (UGC) and Compliance Moderation

Virtual Roll Call allows authorized personnel (department administrators) to publish text and image feeds to facilitate operations. To maintain a secure, professional, and compliant environment:

  • Administrative Oversight: Departmental administrators retain full authority to moderate, hide, or permanently remove any content that violates agency policy, standard operating procedures, or professional conduct codes.
  • Abuse Reporting: Users can flag or report objectionable content and policy violations directly to their organization's department manager or by using our dedicated Support team email listed on the Support Contact page.
