Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Virtual Roll Call collects, uses, and handles your information when you use the Virtual Roll Call mobile application and visit any of our hosted website pages.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We only collect information that is necessary to provide, secure, and maintain our application services.

Profile Information

  • Data Collected: Your name and email address.
  • Purpose: This information is used to uniquely identify you as a user, personalize your experience, and allow you to manage your account.

Device Information and Session Data

  • Data Collected: Your device's IP address.
  • Purpose: We use your IP address to generate a unique session token. This token keeps you securely logged into the app, maintains your active session, and protects your account from unauthorized access.

2. Website Hosting and Technical Data

Our Privacy Policy and application landing pages are hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit these pages, GitHub may automatically collect and store technical information in server log files for security and operation purposes.

  • Information Collected: This information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, internet service provider (ISP) data, and timestamps.
  • Purpose of Collection: GitHub processes this data strictly to maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and ensure website stability.
  • No Marketing Tracking: GitHub does not use this data for marketing or behavioral tracking.
  • Data Privacy: For more details, please review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Data Control, Retention, and Account Deletion

The Virtual Roll Call application is a proprietary tool designed exclusively for use by our authorized clients and their designated personnel. User accounts are provisioned, managed, and maintained strictly by Virtual Roll Call or your organization's designated account administrator.

  • Account Management & Deletion: Because accounts are managed centrally by the organization, users cannot independently delete their profiles from within the app. If your employment, contract, or authorization with the company ends, or if you wish to request the removal of your account, you must contact your organization’s account administrator.
  • Data Retention: Profile information (name and email) and active session tokens are retained on our secure servers only for the duration of your authorized use of the application. Once an account administrator processes an account deletion request or deactivates an employee profile, all corresponding personal identification data and session tokens will be permanently erased from our active databases.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing Disclosure

We value your privacy and maintain strict control over your personal and technical data. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share client user profile information (name and email), device information (IP addresses), or session token data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

  • Internal Infrastructure Only: Your data is processed exclusively within our secure client-data infrastructure to provide and maintain the application's core functions.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: This application does not participate in cross-app tracking. We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Service Providers: We only disclose technical data to our trusted hosting provider (GitHub Pages), as described in Section (2) of this policy, strictly for necessary server operations and security purposes.
